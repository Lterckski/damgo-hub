# Retired: Live Meeting Agenda Board

This unit is intentionally retired.

Damgo Hub is used to schedule meetings and prepare ordered agendas, but the meeting itself takes place in person or through an external meeting service. Do not build a Liveblocks room, React Flow canvas, live notes, cursors, presence, or quote capture for meetings.

Implement the meeting experience described in `16-meeting-scheduling.md`. Accepted agenda proposals are stored and ordered in PostgreSQL and displayed as the final agenda on the normal meeting detail page.
